Privacy Policy

Parami Fitness User App  |  Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Parami Fitness User App (“the App”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The App is operated by Parami Fitness [legal business name] (“Parami Fitness”, “we”, “us”).

In short:

We use your information only to run your gym membership in the App. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. Who can use the App

The App is for members of Parami Fitness. Your account is created by gym staff, and you sign in with the login we give you. The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

2. Information we collect

Account and membership information

When you sign in and use the App, we process the details held on your member account. Depending on what the gym has recorded, this can include:

  • Your name and login (username)
  • Member ID
  • Phone number, email address and home address
  • Your packages, enrolled classes, booked free classes and loyalty points
  • Your check-in history

QR codes and camera

The App shows a personal QR pass that gym staff scan to identify you. If you choose to check in by scanning the gym’s QR code, the App asks for permission to use your camera. The camera is used only to read that QR code. We do not take, save or upload photos or videos.

Location

[CONFIRM AND EDIT: The App may ask for your device location to confirm you are at the gym when you check in. Location is used only at that moment, only while the App is open, and is not used to track you in the background.] [If the App does not use location, delete this section.]

Information stored on your device

The App saves small settings on your device, such as your chosen theme, light or dark mode, and your sign-in session, so you do not have to set them up every time. This stays on your device and is removed when you sign out or uninstall the App.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect payment card details in the App.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising tools in the App. [CONFIRM]

3. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and show your member profile
  • To show classes and the classes you are enrolled in or have booked
  • To display your QR pass and record your gym check-ins
  • To show your packages and loyalty points
  • To keep the App secure, fix problems and prevent misuse
  • To respond when you contact us

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it.

4. How information is shared

Your information is sent between the App and Parami Fitness’s own servers over an encrypted connection. We share it only in these cases:

  • Service providers that host our systems and process data for us under our instructions. [Name your hosting provider if you wish.]
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights, safety or property of our members, staff or the public.
  • Business changes, such as a sale or transfer of the gym business, in which case your information would continue to be protected under this policy.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your membership information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for record keeping, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and restricted staff access. No system is completely secure, so please keep your login private and tell us if you think your account has been misused.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date
  • Delete your account and information, subject to records we must keep by law

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. You can also turn off camera or location access at any time in your device settings. Doing so may stop QR check-in from working.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law [for example, Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) – confirm with your legal adviser]. We will honor those rights.

8. Account deletion

Accounts are created and managed by Parami Fitness. To delete your account and the data linked to it, contact us at [support email] or ask at the gym front desk. We will confirm once it is done.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also let you know in the App or at the gym.
