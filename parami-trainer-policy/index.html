Privacy Policy for Parami Trainer

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Parami Trainer is a fitness trainer application developed by Core Axis Technologies for authorized trainers and fitness staff. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application and its connected services.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled for your account, we may process your name, account identifier, username, email address or phone number, authentication credentials or tokens, assigned classes and schedules, class participant details, attendance or check-in records, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service. The exact information available depends on your role and the fitness organization using the app.

2. Camera and QR Code Scanning

The application may request access to your device camera to scan QR codes or barcodes for supported fitness operations. Camera access is used when you initiate a scanning feature. Scanned code values may be sent to the connected backend to validate or record an operation. Camera access can be changed in your device settings. We do not intentionally use camera access to record video or take photos for unrelated purposes.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to sign users in, manage trainer access, show class assignments and timetables, support class participation and attendance, validate scans, maintain service functionality, troubleshoot issues, and protect accounts from unauthorized access.

4. Local Device Storage

The application may store limited settings or session-related information on your device to maintain your login or preferences. You can clear application data using your device's settings; doing so may sign you out.

5. Backend Systems and Service Providers

Information needed to operate the app is exchanged with the fitness organization's backend systems. Hosting, infrastructure, and other service providers may process information on our behalf to deliver the service. We do not sell personal information. Any additional sharing must be limited to authorized operational needs, legal requirements, or situations described to users.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect account and operational information, including access controls and appropriate technical safeguards. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet operational requirements, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable law. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting the fitness organization managing your account or the privacy contact below. Some records may need to be retained where legally required.

8. Your Choices

You may control camera permissions in your device settings. You may also request information about your personal data, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion where applicable. Disabling certain permissions may limit related app features.

9. Children's Privacy

Parami Trainer is intended for authorized fitness trainers and staff, not for independent use by children. If you believe a child's personal information has been handled improperly, please contact us.

10. International Processing

Depending on where the fitness organization and its service providers operate, information may be processed in jurisdictions other than your own, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this address with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Core Axis Technologies through the official support contact provided by your fitness organization or in the app's App Store listing.
